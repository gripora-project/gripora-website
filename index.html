Stability control for RC cars.

Gripora is open-source firmware that turns a small flight-controller board into a car's chassis electronics: a gyro that holds the line you steer, ABS that keeps the wheels turning under braking, and traction control that puts the power down without the wheelspin.

Early development: not ready to drive yet

What it will do

Steering

Gyro steering assist

Yaw-rate control catches slides and corrects for changing grip, with gain set from the transmitter.

Braking

ABS

Modulates the brake to stop wheel lock, using motor RPM from ESC telemetry.

Throttle

Traction control

Limits wheelspin by comparing wheel speed with how the car is actually moving.

Launch

Launch control

Repeatable starts, with throttle and brake curves you can shape.

Setup

Configurator & radio Lua

Set up and tune from the desktop, a browser, or right on FrSky Ethos and EdgeTX transmitters.

Data

Blackbox logging

High-rate logs of every sensor and output, to tune and diagnose what the car is doing.
